~/about

Backend engineer, cloud-minded builder

I'm a Python backend developer with 2+ years of hands-on experience designing and shipping REST APIs, primarily with FastAPI and Flask. My work sits at the intersection of clean backend architecture and practical cloud engineering — building services that are easy to reason about and infrastructure that doesn't fall over at 3am.

Day to day, that means designing microservices, modeling data with SQLAlchemy across PostgreSQL and MySQL, containerizing services with Docker, and orchestrating data workflows on AWS — including Managed Workflows for Apache Airflow (MWAA), where I've configured environments, debugged execution-role and service-access issues, and written production startup scripts for worker nodes.

I care about clean, typed, testable code, and I treat problem-solving as the actual job — the framework is just a tool. I'm continuously learning: reading AWS documentation, breaking my own side-projects, and picking up whatever the next production bug decides to teach me.
